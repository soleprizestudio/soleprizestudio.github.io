Where did this week's Korean lottery jackpot come from?

Lotto 1pan shows you the retailers that sold this week's first-prize tickets, published every week, on a map you can read at a glance.

## Features

- Find the nearest retailers that sold a first-prize ticket this week
- Get directions to them on the map
- Watch an ad to also see second- and third-prize retailers

Before you buy a ticket, take a look at where this week's luck started.
