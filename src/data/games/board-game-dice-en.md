Lost the dice, or left them at home? This is a dice roller you can open in a browser and use straight away — nothing to install. Roll it right here.

## Presets by game

Every board game wants a different handful of dice. Instead of setting the count and type each time, the common ones are already there.

- **Board games**: Catan, Monopoly, Ludo / Blue Marble, Machi Koro, Backgammon, Risk (attack / defence)
- **Dice games**: Yahtzee, Farkle, Bunco, Las Vegas, Can't Stop, Tenzi, Qwixx, Shut the Box, Perudo (Liar's Dice)
- **TRPG**: full D&D set, d20, d20 with advantage, percentile (d10 ×2)

Playing something that isn't listed? **Create a new template** and save your own dice setup.

## Everything else

- **Shake to roll** — shake your phone and the dice go. A little closer to rolling the real thing.
- **History** — check what you just rolled without anyone having to remember.
- **Total** — no adding up several dice in your head.
- **English / 한국어**

Your setup is saved on the device you're using, with no account needed.
